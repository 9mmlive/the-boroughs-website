RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

The official rules and community guidelines for Boroughs Roleplay. Read and understand them before participating in the city.

This is the start of the rules for Boroughs Roleplay. Make sure to learn the rules before playing Boroughs Roleplay for the first time. Ignorance of the rules will not be tolerated. It is your responsibility to keep up to date with the rules and community guidelines. Any form of violation of the rules listed below will result in either a warning, kick, or a ban. Any decisions made by the staff are final and can take action without a player report. This is an English-speaking server, if you are not a fluent English speaker, this may not be the best place for you as you need to be able to communicate if any issues should arise. Please make sure to have your voice chat working as typing RP makes the RP experience harder on other roleplayer.

Basic Rules

  • There is a zero-tolerance policy for ooc/real life conversation in-game.
  • Roleplay comes first in every situation, and you must remain in character at all times. If there is a problem (including rule breaks, any glitches in game, or any problems that may occur in the server), roleplay the scenario out and then bring it up afterwards by making a ticket in our discord. Make sure to clip the situation for a smoother experience. Please also make sure to have applications that allow you to clip at least 5 minutes of gameplay, for example Medal, Twitch etc.
  • Use common sense. If you are not role playing to a standard that is acceptable in the community you will risk losing your whitelist. If you are uncertain about a certain action you perform on the server, refrain from doing it, keep in character and get support in the discord.
  •  Corruption in Whitelisted roles is not allowed unless the story arc is approved by staff.
  • Keep the drama away from the server. OOC harassment/sexism/idiocy will not be tolerated.
  • No use of ANY Third-Party Applications that may improve your performance. For example, third party hacks, any form of modding etc.
  • If someone breaks a rule, DO NOT GO and break a rule to get revenge on the person that broke a rule to ruin your RP experience. You should either clip it or figure out who did it and inform the staff and they will deal with it.
  • Speed boosting or using any other abnormal movements (zigzags, body rolls, etc) during warfare is not allowed.
  • Any of these locations mentioned are known as GREEN ZONES. This is where killing is not&#x20;&#x70;ermissible at any time. Expect punishment for any killings in these locations: Hospitals, Clothing Stores, Tattoo shops, Food Shops, Places of Business, Garages, Airports, Dubai Island, Religious Places, Shopping centres.

If someone you want to initiate with someone currently in a green zone, you are not allowed to carry them out of the greenzone for your own benefit. You also cannot initiate with people outside or in any car parking / outdoor spaces near a greenzone

In-Game Rules:

1.  &#x20;&#xA0;Random Deathmatch (RDM) / Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Random Deathmatch (RDM) / Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM) is not allowed. RDM is the act of killing or hurting another player or damaging their property intentionally by use of force without having a valid in-character reason to do that. VDM is the act of killing, hurting another player or damaging their property (damaging property can only be done during war with another gang but you cannot run them over, ask staff if not sure) intentionally by using a vehicle without having a valid in-character reason to do that.

2.    Initiation&#xA0;

2a) You need to have INITIATED contact with someone at some point and have a reason for harming someone. You need to say something before you shoot someone with an RP reason otherwise it will be known as RDM, Unless you are in opposing gangs.

(e.g. if someone is selling “drugs” in an area contested by gang members, the gang goes to “ask who they are”,  if they do not cooperate, they have a reason to shoot them)

2b) If you are in a gang, you must not shoot or do any criminal activity against a civilian who are not gang members unless there is a RP reason.

(e.g. a non-gang member has done an activity or action that may anger gang members)

3.    Value Life

You must always VALUE YOUR LIFE and the lives of others. For example, having a gun pointed to you and you do not have a gun taken out originally, being outnumbered (6/1 ratio), you have a knife pointed to you and you do not have any items taken out for self-defence etc. If the person has their gun drawn and not pointed at you, this does not apply. If you are in a vehicle and someone points a gun at you, you do not have to (but you should value your life for RP reason) value your life as you have a vehicle as protection and you can speed off.

4.   METAGAMING&#xA0;

No METAGAMING. Metagaming includes, but is not limited to, using knowledge that your character would not know in the server or communicating information through third party places - e.g., steam chats, discord calls etc. For example, communicating through third party places when you are arrested and placed in cuffs, telling your friends where you are and asking for help

4a) Forms of information your character cannot use without in city knowledge:

  • You may not watch a stream of someone playing Boroughs Roleplay while you yourself are also in-game.
  • You may not use information from any videos or streams of Boroughs Roleplay that your character has no knowledge about
  • You cannot use information from the in-game feed on our discord server or any streams or youtube videos etc
  • You cannot radio or phone call your friends when placed in cuffs.

4b) Forms of information your character can use without in city knowledge:

  • City Documentaries
  • City Podcasts / Music Videos

5.    FAIL RP/ POWERGAMING

No FAIL RP and no POWERGAMING. Fail RP is, but is not limited to, actions that are unrealistic or hurt the roleplay experience of other players. For example, abusing game mechanics (dropping items on the ground, F2, instead of putting into the glovebox, Y, when stopped by police or while being chased by police or anyone), dropping items while being cuffed/arrested, excessive trolling, forcing unwanted RP, Police baiting, combat logging, combat storing, OOC chatting/talking. Fail RP can be intertwined with other rules.

POWERGAMING is roleplaying with mechanics/abilities that do not exist or forcing someone else into a situation where they do not have choices. For example, RPing outside of your character traits, abusing mechanics, using means not actually in-game to gain an advantage. Powergaming can be intertwined with other rules. Roleplaying something unrealistically ridiculous or forcing roleplay actions on other players by using the chat box (‘T’) or /me command is not allowed and is classed as powergaming.

While you are in a chase or stopped by police or any person who is chasing you, you are not allowed to drive to the garages and store your car away to pull out another. That is the same as deleting your car ruining the RP experience of the people around you for your selfish needs. If you are stopped by police or any person who wants to a criminal activity or action to a person on top of the garage, you are not to store your vehicle away. All these and more examples not include in this can be classed as powergaming.

Any sort of SHOOTING POLICE is not allowed. You are to try to escape the police in any possible. Read Rule 12 for a more in detail description. If you start a shootout scene and then you run into your house mid scene to teleport away this is powergaming. If you use the teleporting system of your house to take advantage of a shooting scene to win your gun fight by teleporting in and out is powergaming and abusing game mechanics. You will receive a minimum 3 days ban and the more you do it the higher it goes. You should only be using the th (take hostage) command for rp scenes where it makes sense. It should not be used to stop someone from completely moving and not giving them the option to move. This is abusing mechanics, powergaming and fail RP. People who use this for things like mid gun fight to stop someone from moving, to force someone into a certain roleplay that the other player does not want to be involved in and many other more examples not specifically these two examples (please be smart and try not to find ways to use and abuse /th mechanic other than good roleplay scenes where it makes sense), you will receive a 3 day ban minimum or more depending on the severity of the case.

6.    WARS & HEISTS

A MAXIMUM of 6 people in a single group can be involved in a hostile situation (crime, gunfight etc.) at any given time. You may have more on the roster (gang, or crime group) however they cannot partake in activities beyond the count of 6. If more than 6 are involved, that will count as breaking this rule. Randomly helping a criminal without you knowing who that person is, can be classed at third partying. If you know that person/s involved in a hostile situation, you cannot just go to them and help them, you need to figure out if you can help them without involving yourself in the situation. For example, you can do that using the in-game phone by calling them. If they want your help, they will tell you to join in but both parties need to make sure it is not over 6 individuals in the hostile situation. If they do not ask for your help, you should stay away from the situation and not keep following. That could be seen as third partying or if it is a police chase, Police baiting and third partying. Robbing stores or banks are limited to these numbers of people: 3 for a regular store, 4 for a bank, 5 for jewellery store and 6 for GPS hack.

7.   HOSTAGES/KIDNAPS

Hostages must be GENUINE and not be a willing participant with the hostage takers. Both parties will be found at fault if hostages are not genuine. For example, if the hostage is friends with you in character, hostage is a member of your gang etc. However, your friend in character could turn into your enemies if you take them as a hostage. This could lead to a bigger RP scenario, but you cannot make them a participant in the crime by giving them the loot, this would be a rule break.

8.    AFTER DEATH (NEW LIFE RULE/NLR)

If you have become incapacitated, the NEW LIFE RULE (NLR) is triggered. Getting yourself downed is not considered as dying. You must RP your injuries. If you were brought to the hospital and you respawn at the hospital, you may remember the events that led up to your incapacitation. If your body is not at a hospital before you respawn by pressing ‘E’, you will not remember the immediate events leading up to you being downed and you will lose your items as well.

This is important regarding when you are downed:

8a) You DO NOT respawn while there is an active situation around you. For example, you are getting robbed and the people who downed you have not yet left the scene, POLICE/EMS arrive at the scene which means you DO NOT respawn no matter what as this will count as FAIL RP even if they take a long time taking you to the hospital, if someone is taking you to the hospital you DO NOT respawn as there is RP occurring and many more examples.

8b) If you purposely get killed by a player, you CANNOT take them to a hospital so that you can wait for them to get back up to interrogate them or continue RP. Either leave them where they lay for Police/EMS to help or drop them off at the hospital and leave if you want them to remember the events when they recover.

8c) If there are no Police/EMS around to help the downed people, it will depend on the person who downed you on whether you still remember the events leading up to your incapacitation or not. They should take you to the hospital and leave you there and if they do not take you to the hospital, it means they do not want you to remember the events.

8d) If you respawn, you should STAY AWAY from the area you went down for as long as you can without affecting RP. If you are downed in a scenario and the scenario has ended, you are NOT ALLOWED to return to the area of the scene to collect evidence, loot bodies etc.

8e) After combat RP, you are to take a 30-minute break from killing each other no matter what.

9.    QUITTING/LEAVING (COMBAT LOGGING)

Quitting while in an active situation is NOT ALLOWED, in any way shape or form. You do not quit to benefit you and your needs. If you are downed in an active situation (for example, police downed you) and you leave the server as you do not want to go to jail, expect a punishment if you do not return. If you do quit and you return to the server straight after, you must go back to the situation you were in and continue the RP. If you leave the RP scenario when you come back to the server to avoid it, expect punishments. The same is for game crashes, Wi-Fi disconnects etc.

10.   GTA DRIVING

Taking jumps in vehicles with SIGNIFICANT drops will be considered NVL (No Value Life). For example, going at very high speeds from the motorway (highway) onto a small uphill surface causing you to fly very far and high. It will mean you will be very high off the ground. Driving onto staircases and using it as a getaway is not allowed.

11.   VOICE CHANGERS

You may only use voice changer if you use this voice changer all the time for the specific character, or if you are using it over the phone to remain anonymous for a RP scenario, or if you are trying to anonymously sell items to other role-players to hide your true identity for RP’s sake.

12.   CRIMINAL VS POLICE

Killing police is NOT ALLOWED In real life, the last thing someone would do is shoot at police as they would do anything to escape and lower the chances of dying or going to jail. Role-players should have the same mentality as you would in real life. No one is allowed to steal a taser or guns from any Police Officers and Police Officers are not allowed to hand out any tasers or guns out to other players (this is Corruption in the case of Police Officers). Civs / Crew members are also not allowed to use the tasers to taze someone.This causes an unfair playing field for all players who are not Police Officers. Only police officers are allowed to have and use tasers.

13.  LOOTING

If you kill someone due to RP reasons and you loot their body and you find house keys or any sort of keys on them, you are able to take them but there is one condition. You have to keep the keys on you and not hide it away in your personal house or any type of storage that no one can get access to (i.e., car boot, car glove box, vehicle stored in garage, warehouse etc.) You should carry the keys in your inventory to allow for the opposition to get their house keys back creating more RP. If you are going to make someone not remember the events leading up to their incapacitation, you are not allowed to take the keys from their inventory.

14. STORAGE / ITEMS / WEAPONS

Any items placed in any sort of storage, it is YOUR RESPONSIBILITY if the items are all LOST. For example, storing items in a house and then selling the house with the items will be your responsibility if you lose it. There are other examples but any sort of storage will be classed under this.

15.   SERVER RESTARTS

You may not commit any crime in the 30 minutes leading up to a server restart e.g. killing someone, robbing someone, robbing houses, selling drugs and many more.

16.   PROHIBITED LANGUAGE

You may not commit any IRL disrespect or verbal abuse to other players or staff. Disrespecting dead relatives, parents etc is strictly forbidden.
